# Privacy Policy — Daily Work Tracker **Last updated:** October 13, 2026 ## 1. Introduction Daily Work Tracker helps users record daily tasks, projects, assets, estimated hours, and actual hours worked. ## 2. Information We Access If you choose to sign in with Google, the application may access your Google account information and Google Drive files within the permissions you grant. The application uses this access to identify your account and save or retrieve your work-tracking data. ## 3. How Information Is Used Information is used to provide work tracking features, including task history, hours calculations, monthly summaries, and data synchronization. ## 4. Data Storage Work-tracking records are intended to be stored in the signed-in user's Google Drive. The application does not intentionally share one user's records with other users. ## 5. Data Sharing We do not sell users' personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except as necessary to provide the application, comply with legal obligations, or as authorized by the user. ## 6. Data Security We rely on Google authentication and the permissions granted by users to access Google services. Users should keep their Google accounts secure and review the permissions they grant. ## 7. Data Deletion and Revoking Access Users can delete their work-tracking data and revoke the application's Google access through their Google Account security settings. Deleting data from the application may not automatically delete any separate copies or backups. ## 8. Changes to This Policy This policy may be updated when the application changes. The latest version will be available at this URL. ## 9. Contact For privacy questions, contact the application developer using the developer contact email listed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
